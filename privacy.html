Legal

Privacy Policy

How Rapiprint Design Works handles the information you share with us.

Information We Receive

When you use our intake form or contact us by email, you may share details such as your name, contact information, business name, address, project descriptions, and supporting files. Information you submit through the intake form and in email communications may be used to evaluate projects, provide quotes, and communicate with you.

How We Use Your Information

  • Evaluating your project and determining feasibility
  • Preparing and providing quotes
  • Communicating with you about your project, schedule, and delivery
  • Supporting design, prototyping, and manufacturing work you request

We Do Not Sell Your Information

Rapiprint Design Works does not sell customer information.

Files and Records We Retain

Documents, photos, CAD files, STL files, drawings, and communications you send us may be retained for project support, quoting, manufacturing, and record-keeping.

Intake Form and Email

Our intake form does not upload files or transmit your submission to our servers. It prepares a summary that you copy and send to us by email, along with any attachments you choose to include. Information reaches us only when you send that email.

Website Security and Bot Protection

Cloudflare services may be used for website security and bot protection, including human verification on our intake page. Cloudflare may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details in order to provide these services, subject to Cloudflare's own privacy policy.
