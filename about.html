Precision with personality.

Rapiprint Design Works LLC is a South‑Central Iowa design and manufacturing studio. We value practical engineering, quick iteration, and durable results—built by a small team with big attention to detail.

Why Rapiprint

Engineering is a creative act.

We help individuals, farms, and small manufacturers turn ideas into parts that work. From a one-off replacement bracket to a multi-part assembly, our process focuses on clarity, low cost of failure, and parts you can rely on.

Founder Allen J. Ruth brings hands-on machine shop experience and product design know-how. We keep overhead low, communicate clearly, and ship parts that stand up to farm and factory work.
