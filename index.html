Our workflow / 01

Clear steps. Better builds.

We combine engineering discipline with rural common sense—fast feedback, honest estimates, and parts that work in the real world.

  1. 1 — Tell us the problem.Send sketches, photos, measurements, or just a description. Email, upload, or start the intake form.
  2. 2 — We propose a plan.We respond with scope, materials, and a clear cost and timeline.
  3. 3 — Iteration & build.Prototype, test, and refine until the part works. Then we deliver finished parts or production-ready files.
